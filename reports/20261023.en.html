Outlier-resistant cross-sectional metric: Z = (X - Median) / (1.4826 * MAD), preventing penny-stock anomalies from skewing cross-sectional statistics.

9-point financial health index assessing profitability, leverage, and operating efficiency. Scores >= 5 represent solid structural health.

Credit distress formula evaluating bankruptcy risk. Scores > 2.60 denote safe zones, while < 1.50 trigger balance sheet distress warnings.
